A weighted network is not just a bigger unweighted one.

The top of the weighted network

Aristotle is not just popular. He is repeatedly cited.

Strength counts the total weight of a philosopher's links. It is different from degree: a page can have fewer neighbors and still be cited intensely.

Explore / Overlapping communities

Which tradition is Aristotle really in?

Change how tightly connected a circle must be, then select a community to trace Aristotle’s ties. A k-clique community joins complete groups of k philosophers sharing k − 1 members.

Answer / Aristotle’s tradition

A shared reference, not one exclusive tradition.

At k = 5, circles around Avicenna, Aquinas, and Husserl suggest different strands of Aristotle’s reception. At k = 3 or 4, his memberships merge into one broad community. These are dense reference patterns, not proof of philosophical agreement.
