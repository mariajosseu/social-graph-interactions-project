Question 01 / The filterLOCAL IMPORTANCE

A weighted network is not just a bigger unweighted one.

Keep a link when it matters to at least one end.

The disparity filter asks whether a link takes an unusually large share of a node's total strength. That keeps a minor philosopher's important tie even when a global weight threshold would erase it.

Experiment 03 / Strong tiesWHO GETS THE WEIGHT?

The top of the weighted network

Aristotle is not just popular. He is repeatedly cited.

Strength counts the total weight of a philosopher's links. It is different from degree: a page can have fewer neighbors and still be cited intensely.

Answer / 004

The backbone is a choice, not a revelation.

At α = 0.20, the local filter keeps more than a simple global threshold would for some peripheral philosophers, while the full graph still tells the story of eight broad traditions. We can draw a cleaner history, but we should say exactly which links we chose to forget.
