What this can say

Structure is
not a story.

The friendship paradox is real in this snapshot: following links preferentially lands on well-known characters. Spider-Man, Hulk, and Wolverine drive much of that visibility, but the effect is not only one superstar; degree-weighted sampling makes every popular node easier to encounter.

The shuffle keeps the degree sequence, so the 3.87× popularity gap is expected to survive. Reciprocity falls because mutual pairs are not preserved. We cannot infer motives, chronology, or influence from Wikipedia links: this is a pattern in an editorial network, not a census of friendships.
